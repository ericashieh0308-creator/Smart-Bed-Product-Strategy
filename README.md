# Smart Bed Product Strategy & Project Management

## Objective
This project focuses on designing and developing a smart bed product that integrates multi-sensor technology and AI-driven sleep analytics. The goal is to improve sleep quality through personalized insights and intelligent system design.


## Project Overview
- AI-powered smart bed with sleep monitoring and reporting
- Multi-sensor data collection (pressure, temperature, bio-signals)
- Mobile app integration for control and visualization
- Cloud-based data processing and personalized reports


## My Contributions
- Conducted market research and user persona analysis  
- Performed feasibility analysis (technical, cost, ROI, and risk)  
- Designed Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) and project timeline  
- Developed project roadmap from concept to product launch  
- Contributed to communication planning and stakeholder alignment  



## Key Components

### 1. Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)
- Built a multi-level WBS to organize project tasks  
- Covered areas including design, development, testing, and launch  
- Clearly defined responsibilities across cross-functional teams  


### 2. Project Timeline
- Phase 1: Research & Concept  
- Phase 2: Product & Technical Design  
- Phase 3: Hardware & Software Development  
- Phase 4: Testing & Certification  
- Phase 5: Production & Launch  



### 3. System Architecture
- Sensors → Firmware → AI → Cloud → Mobile App  
- Designed end-to-end data flow and system integration  

---

### 4. Business Strategy
- Subscription-based business model  
- Focus on personalized health insights  
- Positioned for smart home and health-tech markets  

---

### 5. Risk Management
- Identified technical, operational, and supply chain risks  
- Proposed mitigation strategies such as multi-supplier sourcing and early testing  



## Key Insights
- Cross-functional coordination is critical in complex product development  
- Early-stage testing reduces downstream risks and costs  
- Integration between hardware and AI systems is a major challenge  
- User experience plays a key role in product adoption  



## Future Improvements
- Incorporate real-time health monitoring features  
- Enhance AI model accuracy with larger datasets  
- Expand integration with smart home ecosystems
